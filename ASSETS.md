# NINJA — Asset & Visual Reference Policy

## Visual direction
The world follows a realistic nighttime feudal-Japan direction: lantern streets, timber houses, stone paths, mist, forest cliffs, bridges and shrine gates.

Search-engine images and commercial-game screenshots are used only as visual references and are not redistributed in the game.

## Redistributable sources
- Kenney Nature Kit — CC0.
- Poly Haven — CC0.

Only assets with a verified permissive license should be imported into the repository.
